BallBlox Party — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy describes how CrazyBlox ("we") handles information in the game BallBlox Party (the "Game").

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, location or contacts. The Game stores your progress (coins, purchased skins and balls, unlocked islands, quests, settings and the player name you type) on your device or through the platform you play on, so you can continue playing.

Advertising

The Game shows ads, including optional rewarded ads. Ads are provided by third-party ad networks, which may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and usage data to show and measure ads. Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies. On Android you can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Online play

If you choose online play, your player name, selected skin and in-game position are shared with other players in the same match. A short room code is temporarily stored on our server to help players find each other; it contains no personal data and expires within minutes.

Analytics and crash reports

The Game or the platform may collect anonymous technical data (device model, OS version, crashes, gameplay events) to keep the Game working and improve it.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can delete your progress by clearing the app data or uninstalling the Game. For any privacy request, contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
